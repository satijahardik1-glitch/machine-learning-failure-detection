What the scores mean

The failure score comes from a gradient-boosted tree model trained on 7,500 rows. The unusualness score comes from an Isolation Forest that only saw normal rows. A failure alert wins; otherwise a high unusualness score marks the reading as anomalous.

How well it worked

On 2,500 held-out rows the failure model caught 71 of 85 recorded failures (recall 0.84) with precision 0.57 and false alarms on 2.2% of healthy rows. The unusualness check alone found only 1 in 5 failures.

Read with care

The training data is synthetic (AI4I 2020), the score is not a calibrated probability, and an alert is a prompt to inspect, not a diagnosis. The curves show the failure score as one sensor changes and the others stay put.
